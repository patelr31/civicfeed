      How this was made

      1. Data: all comments on the docket, from the public Mirrulations archive of regulations.gov, including text extracted from PDF attachments.
      2. Rules, in code: form-letter and template campaigns (shared wording), commenter type, stance from explicit wording, key recommendation sentences, and TF-IDF clustering of those sentences into candidate themes.
      3. AI step: Claude labelled and merged the clusters, wrote neutral summaries and picked quotes, following ANALYZE.md. It wrote no numbers.
      4. Checks, in code: every quote must appear word for word in the cited comment, or the build fails. Every count and debate split is a pattern match over distinct comments.
